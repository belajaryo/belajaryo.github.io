Who we are

Belajar Yo! (@belajaryo.id) is an independent Indonesian short-form education channel. Contact: belajaryo.id@gmail.com.

What the posting tool does

"Belajar Yo Post" is a private tool that runs on the creator's own computer. It uploads videos that Belajar Yo! made to Belajar Yo!'s own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads and TikTok, using each platform's official API. It is not offered to the public, and only the Belajar Yo! creator signs in to it.

Data we access

  • Basic profile information of our own accounts (account id, username, display name), to confirm we post to the right account.
  • The permission to upload and publish videos, captions and comments to those accounts.

We do not access followers' personal data, private messages, contacts or analytics of other users.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the creator's computer, in a private file readable by that user account. They are sent only to the platform that issued them. We use no analytics, cookies or trackers on this website or in the tool.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until they expire or until we disconnect the tool. You can ask us to delete any data related to you by emailing belajaryo.id@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You can also revoke the tool's access at any time from your Google, Meta or TikTok account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
